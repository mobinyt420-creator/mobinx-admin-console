Account & Data Deletion

MOBIN X — Gaming Ecosystem (Google Play Compliance)

Permanent Action: Requesting account deletion will permanently remove your MOBIN X gamer profile, authentication records, and associated data. This action cannot be reversed.

1. What Data is Deleted

When your account deletion is processed, the following information is permanently erased from our active databases:

  • Your full profile, including Name, Email, Phone Number, and Free Fire UID.
  • Firebase Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens.
  • Tournament history and lobby registrations.
  • Saved custom sensitivity presets and personal app configurations.
  • In-app notifications and wallet points balance.

2. Legitimate Data Retention

Certain minimal system server logs may be retained temporarily (up to 30 days) solely for fraud detection, legal compliance, and security audits, after which they are automatically purged.

3. How to Delete Your Account In-App

If you currently have the MOBIN X app installed on your Android device:

  • Open the MOBIN X app and navigate to Profile.
  • Tap Account Settings → Delete Account.
  • Confirm your decision in the prompt to immediately wipe your account.
