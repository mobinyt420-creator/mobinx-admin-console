Privacy Policy for MOBIN X

Last Updated: August 28, 2026 | Effective Date: August 28, 2026
Google Play Data Safety Compliant

MOBIN X ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and associated web services collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the MOBIN X Gaming Ecosystem application.

1. Introduction

MOBIN X is a gaming ecosystem and community application providing gaming-related tools, free community tournaments, gaming resources, game sensitivity calculators, downloads, and related web-based companion services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, MOBIN X may process:

  • Full Name & Username: To identify your gamer profile in community tournaments and leaderboards.
  • Email Address: Used for account creation, login, password recovery, and service communications.
  • Phone Number: Used for account identity and tournament coordinator contact.
  • Google Account Information: Name, Email, and Avatar received during Google Sign-In.
  • Firebase Authentication Identifier (UID): Unique primary identifier assigned to your authenticated account.
  • User-Created Data: Saved gaming sensitivity presets, joined tournament records, and app preferences.

3. Google Sign-In

When Google Sign-In is enabled and used, authentication is handled securely via official Google OAuth APIs and Firebase Authentication. MOBIN X never requests, receives, or stores your Google account password.

4. Email / Password Authentication

When manual authentication is enabled, your email and password are submitted directly to Google Firebase Authentication. Passwords are never stored as plaintext in our databases or device storage.

5. Use of Information

Information processed by MOBIN X is used exclusively for:

  • Creating and managing your gamer profile.
  • Authenticating your access across sessions.
  • Facilitating participation in free gaming tournaments and room coordination.
  • Saving user-generated sensitivity configurations and app settings.
  • Providing customer support and security alerts.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and automated bot activities.

6. Firebase Services

MOBIN X utilizes official Google Cloud Firebase services (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Storage) for real-time cloud data storage and authentication. All network requests are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS).

7. Shop & External Web Services

MOBIN X may provide in-app browser links to external web stores or community platforms. Users interacting with third-party external websites are subject to those independent websites' own privacy policies and terms of service. MOBIN X does not collect or process banking details from external web stores.

8. Data Security

We apply robust technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS encryption, restricted database access rules, and authenticated Firebase tokens, to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

9. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is processed solely through certified cloud infrastructure providers (Google Cloud & Firebase) necessary to deliver core app functionality.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal profile data only for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, your personal data and associated records are permanently erased from our active databases.

11. Account Deletion (In-App & Web)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, MOBIN X provides two convenient mechanisms to delete your account and associated data:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account to permanently erase your profile and credentials immediately.
  • Public Web Resource: Visit our official deletion page at https://mobinx-admin-console.vercel.app/delete-account.html to submit an account deletion request online.

12. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MOBIN X is designed for a general gaming audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that personal data of a child under 13 has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete it.

13. User Rights

You hold the right to access, review, update, or permanently delete your personal information stored with MOBIN X at any time through the in-app profile settings or by contacting our support team.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
