Privacy Policy for Mobin X

Last Updated: August 28, 2026 | Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Mobin X ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and services collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Mobin X - Gaming Hub application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal, necessary information required to provide gaming utility services, tournament registration, and account authentication:

  • Personal Information: Full Name, Email Address (via Google Sign-In), Phone Number, and Free Fire UID.
  • Authentication Data: Google OAuth credentials and secure Firebase Authentication tokens.
  • Device and App Analytics: Device model, OS version, and app performance logs to prevent crashes and optimize gaming features.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage your gamer profile and wallet balance.
  • To register you into Free Fire custom tournaments (Clash Squad 4v4, Solo BR).
  • To calculate and save custom game sensitivity profiles.
  • To provide customer support and notify you of important app updates and match schedules.

3. Data Safety and Protection

Your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS using industry-standard SSL encryption and stored in Google Cloud Firestore backend databases. We implement strict access controls and do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes certified third-party service providers compliant with Google Play policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core app functionalities, updates, and authentication.
  • Google Firebase: Real-time Firestore database and Firebase Authentication.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Mobin X does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can remove such data.

6. User Data Deletion and Your Rights

You have the full right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account and associated data. You may request immediate deletion of your gamer profile and data at any time by emailing us at mobinyt420@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page and providing an in-app notice when appropriate.
